Android not working?

Google's phone app can't add a calendar from a link. Either open this page on a computer signed in to the same Google account and tap the Google button there, or install the free app ICSx⁵ from the Play Store, tap +, and paste this address:

webcal://mattcreamer75.github.io/hoke-crew-calendar/c/JZAzx-N_otjzWaHu/schedule.ics

Add it once. After that your calendar updates itself when the schedule changes (iPhone: within about an hour; Google: can take several hours). This page always shows the latest.

Put this page on your home screen

One tap opens your schedule, like an app. It always shows the latest (needs signal).

iPhone
  1. Open this link in Safari. If it opened inside a text message, tap the Safari or compass button to open it there.
  2. Tap the Share button ↑ (square with an arrow, bottom of the screen).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add (top right). A Hoke Schedule icon appears on your home screen.
Android
  1. Open this link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu ⋮ (three dots, top right).
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  4. Tap Add or Install. A Hoke Schedule icon appears on your home screen.
